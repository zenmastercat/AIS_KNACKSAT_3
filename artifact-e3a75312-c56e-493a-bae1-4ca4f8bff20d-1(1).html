KNACKSAT-3: AIS 162 MHz / UHF 401 MHz coexistence on a 1U

Two crossed dipoles (4 elements) on the +Z face. Antenna coupling is taken from the NEC-2 model (sim/antenna_coupling_nec.py, 95th percentile of 200 deployments). Two separate problems are shown: blocking by the 401 MHz carrier (cured by SAW filters) and in-band UHF noise at 162 MHz (cured by a TX-side filter or time-division; an AIS SAW cannot remove it).
